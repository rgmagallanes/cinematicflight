# Cinematic Flight

## Platform

Responsive marketing website and interactive web experience.

## Audience

Owners and decision-makers for visually distinctive resorts, retreats, villas, farm stays, hospitality venues, and destination properties whose current website does not communicate the quality of the place.

## Purpose

Demonstrate and market a website design and build service whose signature feature is a cinematic, scroll-controlled view of the property created from photographs the client already has. Help a qualified property owner understand the offer, experience a working example, and begin a property discussion.

## Positioning

Cinematic Flight creates property websites and produces a cinematic view of the place as their signature experience. Verified property photographs become the anchor points of an interactive web journey, supported by the surrounding website and its enquiry or booking route.

## Current prototype truth

- The hero uses the verified Good Food Farm flight clips and real photographic anchors.
- Desktop and phone-weight motion assets are selected responsively.
- The enquiry form demonstrates validation and a success state locally; it is not connected to an external endpoint.
- No pricing, performance claims, customer testimonials, availability, booking action, or production promises are present.
- The existing Good Food Farm client prototype outside this folder remains unchanged.

## Primary actions

- Enter and experience the cinematic journey.
- Understand the service in one clear sentence.
- View the photographic proof.
- Discuss a property through the enquiry flow.

## Accessibility and performance commitments

- Semantic headings, navigation, labels, and controls.
- Keyboard-visible focus and a skip link around the long cinematic sequence.
- Responsive layouts down to 320px.
- Reduced-motion visitors receive a static photographic opening.
- Desktop and mobile video weights remain separate.

## Publication gaps

- Connect the enquiry form to an approved endpoint.
- Verify the full motion experience on real iOS Safari, including low-power behavior.
- Replace prototype-specific contact handling with the final business route.
